## App name

## Short description (66/80 chars)
Schedule volunteers and run your Sunday service, all in one place.

## Full description (under 4000 chars)

Declare is the church operating system for teams that schedule volunteers — built to replace the spreadsheet and the group chat.

**Schedule with confidence**
Assign people to roles for each service and send the invite with one tap. Declare automatically checks for scheduling conflicts and blockout dates, so you're never double-booking someone who's already serving elsewhere that day, or asking someone who told you they'd be away.

**See responses as they happen**
Know exactly who's accepted, who's declined, and who still hasn't answered — for every role, every service, at a glance. Resend an invite to anyone who's gone quiet with a single tap.

**Reminders that actually go out**
Declare reminds volunteers by email and text a few days before they're serving, and again the day before if they still haven't responded. No more finding out about an empty role Sunday morning.

**One roster for your whole team**
See everyone in your church, what roles they're able to serve in, and their permission level. Message a single role, a whole team, or one person directly from the app.

**Built for how churches actually run**
Organize roles into teams (Worship, Tech, Hospitality, whatever fits your church), reorder them however makes sense to you, and manage blockout dates for volunteers who let you know in advance when they're unavailable.

Free to get started, no credit card required.
